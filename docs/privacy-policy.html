FAIZ-E-RAZA - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

FAIZ-E-RAZA (Maktab-E-Tajushshriah, Faiz-E-Raza Group Mehkar) is an app used by the Maktab management, teachers, parents and students to manage Islamic education: attendance, syllabus progress, results, fees and notices. It also includes prayer times and a tasbeeh counter that anyone can use.

Information we collect

  • Mobile number - used to sign you in with a one-time password (OTP) and to link parents to their children.
  • Student records entered by the Maktab staff - name, date of birth, father's name, parent mobile number, address, Aadhaar number (where provided), class, attendance, syllabus progress, exam results, fee status and complaints.
  • Staff and monthly member records - name, mobile number, role, attendance/leave and monthly contribution payments recorded by the Maktab.
  • Device notification token - to send you alerts such as absence, fee and course updates.
  • Approximate location - only if you open Prayer Times and allow it, to calculate prayer times and show your place name. Your location is not stored on our servers.

How we use it

Only to run the Maktab's education and administration services: signing you in, showing a parent their own child's records, recording attendance, results and fees, sending notifications, and calculating prayer times. We do not sell your data, and we do not show advertising.

Who can see your information

Access is limited by role. Parents see only their own children. Teachers and Maktab admins see the students of their own Maktab. Super admins and authorised committee members see records needed for management. Monthly members see only their own payment records.

Services we use

  • Supabase - secure database and file storage for app records.
  • Google Firebase - phone number OTP verification and push notifications.
  • Aladhan and OpenStreetMap Nominatim - prayer time calculation and place name from your approximate coordinates, when you use Prayer Times.

Data is sent to these services over encrypted connections.

Children

Student records are entered and managed by the Maktab staff and the child's parent or guardian. Children do not create accounts on their own, and we do not knowingly collect data directly from children.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while a student or member is associated with the Maktab and as needed for the Maktab's records. To request correction or deletion of your or your child's data, contact us at the email below, and we will act on verified requests.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and regular backups. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
