FAIZ-E-RAZA - Delete your account and data

This page explains how to ask for your account, or your child's records, in the FAIZ-E-RAZA app (Maktab-E-Tajushshriah, Faiz-E-Raza Group Mehkar) to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to faizerazagroup@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FAIZ-E-RAZA account".
  2. In the email, write the mobile number you use to sign in to the app and your name. Parents who want a child's records deleted should also write the child's name and Maktab.
  3. We may contact you on that mobile number to confirm that the request is yours.
  4. We will complete verified requests within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and mobile number.
  • Your profile, role and notification settings.
  • For a parent's request: the child's personal details held by the app, such as address and Aadhaar number, and the parent contact number.
  • Notification tokens and in-app alerts for your account.

What may be kept

  • Basic education records that the Maktab needs to keep, such as past attendance totals, results and fee payment receipts, may be kept for the Maktab's records. They are kept without your sign-in account and are used only for the Maktab's own administration.
  • Backups are removed within 90 days of deletion.
